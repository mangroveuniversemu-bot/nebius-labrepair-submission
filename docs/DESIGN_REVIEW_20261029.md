existing synthetic Bell–CHSH repair demonstration. Repository visibility and
publication status are separate delivery decisions.

## Existing animation review

The production review sampled frames from five user-provided local clips. Two
were generic typing interactions; one contained invented interface text. A
document-separation action was useful for the closing distinction. Another clip
had a useful green/red reversal, but its generated numbers were not evidence.
Crop or mask invented text and figures. Treat all character animation as
illustration, not a customer testimonial, hardware experiment, or product capture.
Favor the useful actions rather than preserving every clip at full length.

## Editing and interface direction

Jobs and Bezos are **inspiration lenses**, not endorsements or quoted design
rules: reduce the screen to one clear question, and work backward from the
research engineer's decision and the handover they need.

- Lead with the contradiction: **Two repairs. Same total. One is wrong.** Identify
  the illustrative engineer briefly; do not delay the hook for a biography.
- Compare the candidate's S with the registered 2.60 reference, then expose the
  individual pair checks. Color has a semantic role: pass, reject, or review.
- Keep the original 3.40 claim separate. Repair correctness cannot upgrade it
  from NOT_REPRODUCED or establish a physical interpretation.
- Put the decision and next action above secondary provenance details. Retain
  source bindings, reasons, measured usage, and a downloadable handover.
- Name Nebius Token Factory and NVIDIA Nemotron in the narration. Explain the
  supplied channel convention and the model's bounded parameter-translation job.
- Keep saved real cloud execution separate from curated local controls. The
  revised interface and movie require no fresh provider call or paid resource.
- The new production target is ten scenes in 142 seconds: immediate contradiction,
  actual review, real execution evidence, uncertainty, and an inspectable receipt.
  Final encoded duration and quality require the separate media-verification record.

The result should be judged on a visible decision and its supporting evidence,
not on animation polish, an invented autonomous loop, or unmeasured impact.
