## What the earlier edit missed

The previous 142-second LabRepair film repeated a warm-white presentation frame,
large outer heading, persistent technical header and footer, and static cards.
Character footage was reduced to a narrow portrait while the surrounding slide
retained most of the screen. Composition changed less than the underlying story.
Its opening also marked one repair green and the other red before the comparison,
weakening the question the viewer was supposed to investigate.

VisualGuard's useful lesson is a change in visual grammar with each story beat:
situation, visible problem, action, changed outcome and an inspectable next step.
This is an editorial observation, not evidence that a style caused an award or
that another project's results establish LabRepair's effectiveness.

## New edit direction

The [narration specification](demo_narration_20261030.json) targets **123 seconds
and 12 scenes**. The final export verifies 123 seconds, 1920 x 1080, 25 fps,
33 timed shots, 38 ordered subtitle intervals and twelve fitting male voice clips.
Measured speech is 98.506 seconds. The tight cut targets about 80% narration
coverage, reducing silent holds rather than filling a preset length.
The 33 shots use four visual types; decorative motion is not a substitute
for a visible change in evidence or the reviewer's next action.

- Open on full-width illustrative workplace footage and the engineer's decision.
- Present two neutral 2.60 totals before revealing the conflicting pair evidence.
- Enter the actual interface at second 9; rejection appears at second 14. Show the wrong candidate first, then
  the declared repair, so the viewer observes the reason for different verdicts.
- Use the actual UI at a readable scale without the previous oversized outer
  slide heading. Small source chips distinguish illustration, local replay and
  separately recorded execution.
- Reveal the four pair comparisons progressively. Their values come from the
  existing synthetic case, not a new experiment or an invented visualization.
- Change composition and dark/light treatment as the story moves from a question
  to an interaction, evidence, bounded model proposal and human handover.
- Explain NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory after the viewer has seen the
  problem. The supplied convention and protected inputs remain explicit.
- Return to the person and the source-bound hold, then close with the inspectable
  handover: what changed, what failed and what still needs evidence.
