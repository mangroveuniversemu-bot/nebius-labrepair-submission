## What changed

The film opens with two 2.60 results, then shows how conflicting individual pair
evidence changes the verdict. Selected illustrative character footage supplies
the engineer's role; generated interfaces, figures and signatures are cropped
or masked. The new homepage appears at second 14, followed by actual sample
selection, independent review, explicit uncertainty and a source-bound hold.

The interface now shows the candidate total, registered reference and independent
verdict together. Original claim 3.40 remains separate and NOT_REPRODUCED.
The model section names Nebius Token Factory and NVIDIA Nemotron and explains
that the prompt supplied the convention. The original real generation and
Sandbox execution are saved evidence, not fresh calls made for this film.
